About HandSpeak

HandSpeak aims to make communication more accessible by combining sign recognition, speech processing, and language technologies in a unified platform.

Today the platform focuses on image-based sign letter recognition, powered by a MediaPipe hand-landmark pipeline and Random Forest models for both American and Indian Sign Language. Speech recognition, text-to-speech, translation, and sentence-level communication are planned for future iterations and are not yet operational.
